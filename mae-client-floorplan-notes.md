# Floor Plan Review Notes

Date: August 8, 2026

## Client Brief Summary

Based sa message ni client, ito yung key points:

- Existing residential plan ito as of now, pero ang direction is ipa-renovate at i-convert into an apartment setup.
- Yung lower level or existing portion, gusto nilang minimal changes lang as much as possible.
- Yung second floor, parang fresh build ang direction.
- Yung kitchen side ang planong gawing bagong harapan.
- Yung mga kwarto, gusto nilang as is na lang as much as possible.
- Sa laundry area daw gagawa ng daan.
- May nakaabang na bathroom near the master bedroom na bare pa, at iyon daw ang isa sa importanteng areas na kailangang ma-resolve.

## Initial Planning Read

Overall, clear naman yung direction ni client: keep what is still working, iwas sa unnecessary demolition, then focus sa circulation, access, bathroom planning, at kung paano magiging functional ang layout once converted into an apartment. Practical itong approach lalo na kung gusto nilang controlled yung scope at costing.

Based sa updated plan, may `2 T&B` na naka-label:

- isang `T&B` beside the `Master Bedroom`
- isang `T&B` malapit sa transition ng `Master Bedroom` at `Living` area

Since apartment conversion na ang direction, ang main question ngayon is hindi na lang kung may bathroom ba o wala, kundi:

- alin sa dalawang `T&B` ang unfinished or bare pa
- alin ang intended na maging main bathroom
- paano ito gagamitin in terms of privacy, accessibility, at tenant use
- single apartment ba ito or potentially multiple rental units in one property

Main planning implications:

- Since apartment use na ang target, mas importante na ngayon ang clear access, privacy, and independent usability ng spaces.
- Since yung kitchen side ang magiging harapan, maaapektuhan nito yung entry experience, facade treatment, at overall flow pagpasok ng property.
- Yung bagong daan through the laundry needs to be planned carefully para hindi mag-feel na service entry lang siya, especially if future tenants ang gagamit.
- Keeping the bedrooms as is is good for cost control, pero kailangan pa rin i-check kung okay pa rin ang circulation, privacy, and door movement once finalized na ang apartment access changes.
- Since dalawang `T&B` ang nasa plan, kailangan ma-clarify agad alin talaga ang main bathroom at alin ang secondary or private use.
- Kailangan ding ma-confirm if ang apartment goal is one whole rental unit, owner-occupied with rental portion, or multiple smaller units later on.

## Suggestions and Recommendations

### 1. Clarify the new main entrance setup

Since yung kitchen side ang magiging bagong harapan, magandang ma-define agad:

- ito na ba ang actual main entrance ng apartment
- dito rin ba dadaan ang future tenants or visitors
- okay lang ba sa client na mas visible ang kitchen from the front side

Recommendation:

- If ito na ang magiging main entrance, mas okay kung may konting transition bago dumiretso sa working zone.
- If possible, pwedeng mag-create ng mas intentional na entry feel through a divider, screen, or mas malinaw na path papuntang dining/living.
- If apartment use ito, mas okay kung ang entrance feels neutral and presentable, hindi masyadong direct sa private or service areas.

### 2. Review the laundry access carefully

Workable yung idea na sa laundry area ang daan, pero kailangan ma-check kung magiging maayos pa rin yung experience pagpasok, especially if renters or separate occupants ang gagamit.

Recommendation:

- Check kung yung access path tatama agad sa laundry functions, sampayan, or utility storage.
- If needed, pwedeng i-separate visually or functionally yung passage para mas clean tingnan.
- Important din i-confirm if this is for shared use, tenant use, or daily family use lang.

### 3. Keep the bedrooms as is, but validate if they still work for apartment use

Magandang move yung i-preserve yung bedrooms if okay naman na yung current sizes at placement.

Recommendation:

- Recheck pa rin yung bed layout, cabinet allowance, at walking clearance.
- Confirm if may room na kailangan ng future flexibility for storage, partitioning, or other apartment-related use.
- Check din kung may possible door conflicts sa revised circulation outside the rooms.
- If apartment setup ito, check whether each room still feels practical in relation to shared bathroom, kitchen, and access.

### 4. Confirm the bathroom strategy for apartment use

Ito yung isa sa pinaka-importanteng i-clarify based sa updated plan.

Recommendation:

- Confirm alin sa dalawang `T&B` yung bare or unfinished bathroom na na-mention ni client.
- Confirm if yung `T&B` beside the `Master Bedroom` will stay more private, or if iyon talaga ang gusto nilang gawing main bathroom.
- If gagawin iyong main bathroom for everyone, check if comfortable ba itong i-access ng ibang users or tenants.
- Review plumbing, ventilation, and actual fixture allowance before finalizing.
- If apartment use ang goal, mas okay if the main bathroom is easy to access without entering a highly private bedroom zone.

### 5. Check the kitchen-as-front condition

Since yung kitchen side ang magiging bagong harapan, hindi lang function ang nagbabago, pati overall impression ng house.

Recommendation:

- Confirm if okay lang sa client na more visible ang kitchen area from the exterior.
- Think about how to keep the frontage neat kahit active working kitchen siya.
- Consider privacy, window placement, airflow, heat, and possible clutter exposure from the front.
- If apartment ito, consider whether the front-facing kitchen gives the right impression for a rental setup, or if mas okay na may clearer receiving/common entry feel.

### 6. Clarify the apartment setup itself

Ito yung pinaka-importanteng strategic question bago mag-finalize ng layout.

Recommendation:

- Confirm if the goal is one whole apartment unit, duplex-style use, or multiple rental rooms/units in the future.
- Confirm if may hiwalay bang access needed for upper and lower levels.
- Check if shared ba ang kitchen and bathroom, or if may target silang more independent tenant setup later on.
- This will affect circulation, privacy, plumbing planning, and estimation significantly.

## Questions for the Client

Ito yung mga pinaka-useful na follow-up questions before ma-finalize yung direction:

1. Ang target po ba is one whole apartment unit, or balak ninyo na eventually gawing multiple rental units or rooms?
2. Yung kitchen side po ba ang magiging main entrance ng apartment for both tenants and visitors?
3. Yung daan sa laundry, gusto niyo po ba na iyon na ang primary access, or secondary access lang?
4. Yung existing bedrooms, okay na po ba talaga ang sizes and layout nila for the apartment setup, or ang goal lang muna is to avoid extra changes and cost?
5. Sa updated plan, may 2 `T&B` na naka-indicate. Alin po doon yung bare pa or unfinished na bathroom?
6. Yung `T&B` beside the master bedroom po ba ang gusto ninyong gawing main bathroom?
7. If iyon ang magiging main bathroom, okay lang po ba na iyon ang gamitin ng lahat ng occupants or tenants, or mas gusto niyo ng mas accessible na common bathroom setup?
8. May existing plumbing and drainage provision na po ba doon sa unfinished bathroom area?
9. Sa ground floor, alin po yung talagang ayaw na ninyong galawin?
10. Sa second floor, ang target po ba is additional rentable space, more rooms, or a separate apartment-like level?
11. Kailangan po ba ng hiwalay na access for upper and lower levels?
12. May preferred exact location po ba kayo ng main entrance once ma-orient na sa kitchen side yung harapan?
13. May vehicle size po ba tayong kailangang i-consider for the carport?
14. May target budget range po ba kayo para ma-align natin yung scope ng design and renovation works?

## Suggested Direction to Present to Client

Pwede mong i-frame yung update nang ganito:

`Based sa notes ninyo, ang best direction is to keep the existing lower portion as minimal as possible, preserve the bedrooms where practical, and focus the revisions sa new access through the laundry area, sa kitchen side na magiging harapan, and sa bathroom planning. Since ang goal na pala is ma-convert itong residential layout into an apartment, need ko lang i-confirm a few details on access, bathroom use, and whether the setup is for one whole apartment unit or future multiple rental use para mas aligned yung final layout sa actual use ninyo.`

## Notes for Estimation

For estimation, ito yung likely na pinaka-makakaapekto sa costing:

- possible structural changes for the new access
- wall revisions around the laundry and circulation path
- bathroom completion and plumbing works
- any functional revision between the two `T&B` areas
- frontage or facade adjustments if the kitchen side becomes the new front
- any second-floor scope na ituturing na fresh build
- any revisions needed to make the property work better as an apartment or rental setup

Because of that, mas safe na preliminary pa lang ang estimate until ma-confirm ni client ang:

- final access point
- final bathroom use and setup
- apartment setup type
- extent ng second-floor work
- areas na strictly as is and not to be touched
